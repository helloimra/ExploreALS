## Overview
`ExploreALS v1.0` synthesizes **59 ALS drug and genetic target candidates**, **19 single-gene sub-nodes** de-bundled from multi-gene composite axes, and **26 coordinate-only human `GRCh38` cis-regulatory search windows** (`0` synthetic oligomer sequences) across `48` SHA-256-verified open-access human ALS cohorts (`NYGC GSE153960`, `Pineda 2024`, `Edinburgh GSE290359`, `Zhang 2026` spatial, `Spillman 2026` fraction DIA, `Trautwig 2025` & `Oh 2023` living CSF proteomics, `Brown/Ma 2022` & `Bryce-Smith/Zeng 2025` TDP-43 splicing/APA, and `Bye 2025` sALS patient iPSC-MN drug screening).
